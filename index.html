FAQ

Things people ask at the counter.

Do I need to pay when I book?

No. Reserving holds the bike. You pay at pickup with card or cash, and you can cancel free up to 24 hours before.

What is included with the rental?

A helmet, a U-lock, a bell, a paper map and a flat-repair kit. E-bikes come fully charged with a charger if you keep it overnight.

What are your hours?

Daily 8 am to 6 pm, March through October. 9 am to 5 pm the rest of the year. Returns after hours go in the coded bike room; we will text you the code.

Can kids ride?

Yes. We have kids' bikes from 20" wheels, trailers and tag-alongs. They are not bookable online yet, so call us and we will set them aside.

What if it rains?

It mostly does not. If it does, cancel for free or swap your dates. Fog does not count as rain.
